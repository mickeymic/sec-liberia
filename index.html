About SEC Liberia

Building a greener and more resilient Liberia

The Society for Environmental Conservation (SEC) - Liberia is an independent NGO dedicated to protecting forests, watersheds, and wildlife while creating sustainable opportunities for communities on the frontlines of environmental change.

Mission

Conserve ecosystems through inclusive action and evidence-based programs.

Vision

A Liberia where people and nature thrive together for generations.

Core Values

Integrity, equity, stewardship, innovation, and local leadership.

Our Programs

Key Focus Areas

🌳

Forestry & Reforestation

Restoring degraded lands with indigenous species and community forest governance.

🌍

Climate Change

Scaling adaptation and mitigation initiatives for climate-vulnerable communities.

🌱

Sustainable Agriculture

Promoting climate-smart farming methods that protect soils and improve livelihoods.

💧

Water & Sanitation

Improving safe water access and sanitation systems in underserved communities.

🦜

Wildlife Protection

Safeguarding species habitats through anti-poaching awareness and local action.

📘

Environmental Education

Building eco-literacy through school clubs, civic training, and community awareness campaigns.
